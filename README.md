# MARKET-REGIME — Large-Scale Market Data Research

## Project Overview

MARKET-REGIME is an independent research project focused on
investigating cryptocurrency market behavior using large-scale
historical market data.

The project extends earlier systematic trading research by
developing a structured framework for studying market conditions,
price behavior, and the relationships between different types
of market events.

The primary objective is to organize complex market observations
into reproducible research processes that support systematic
analysis and evidence-based conclusions.

## Research Scope

The research environment includes:

- Historical market data covering more than 500 cryptocurrency instruments.
- Daily and intraday market observations.
- Datasets containing tens of millions of historical records.
- Systematic investigation of market conditions and price behavior.
- Analysis of relationships between market events across different timeframes.
- Statistical evaluation of historical observations.
- Reproducibility and consistency checks.

The project emphasizes large-scale data processing and systematic
research methodology rather than discretionary market interpretation.

## System Architecture

The research framework is organized around several functional components:

**Data Layer**

Historical market data collection, organization, storage,
and consistency verification.

**Research Layer**

Structured investigation of market behavior and historical
market observations.

**Validation Layer**

Verification of research assumptions, historical consistency,
and reproducibility of analytical results.

**Reporting Layer**

Generation of structured research outputs, statistical summaries,
and diagnostic information.

The architecture separates data processing, research logic,
validation, and reporting responsibilities.

## Research Principles

The project follows several core principles:

**Causal Data Processing**

Research calculations use information available at the
corresponding historical point in time.

**Reproducibility**

Research procedures are designed to produce consistent
results when executed under the same conditions.

**Data Integrity**

Historical datasets are checked for consistency and
processing errors.

**Separation of Responsibilities**

Data preparation, research calculations, validation,
and reporting are organized as distinct system components.

**Evidence-Based Evaluation**

Research conclusions are evaluated using historical observations
and explicit validation procedures.
